# Kaiju Cinematic Grammar Skill Pack v1

**Pack ID:** `M_FIELD_KAIJU_CINEMATIC_GRAMMAR_V1`  
**Scope:** 通用巨物／怪獸／巨型機器人／巨人電影語法；跨專案重用。  
**Status:** Active / validating

## Activation Rule

當 Shot 出現以下任一條件時啟動本 Pack：
- giant-scale subject
- kaiju / giant robot
- giant-human spatial relationship
- city-scale impact / destruction
- giant motion / giant spatial sound

## Startup Gate

任務開始前必須：
1. 查 `skills/REGISTRY.yaml`。
2. 挑選本 Shot 必要 Skill，不整包載入。
3. 宣告 `skills_loaded`。
4. 先讀 Project Canon，再執行。
5. 付費生成前跑 Kaiju preflight。

## Non-negotiable Gates

- Physics correctness before spectacle.
- No hovering / arbitrary giant-limb slowdown.
- Camera must have a physical or motivated position.
- Known-size anchors must preserve scale readability.
- Motion / Look / Identity / Voice references are assigned distinct responsibilities.
- All readable text must be correct; no gibberish.
- Camera movement must not create or delete crowd members.
- Environmental reaction must follow cause, travel time, inertia and damping.
- Full-body reveal is not mandatory at close range; partial reveal is often stronger.
- Low-resolution validation before high-resolution final generation.

## Research Roots

This pack distills production methods studied from professional giant-creature filmmaking, including *Godzilla* (2014), *Pacific Rim*, *Kong: Skull Island*, *King Kong* (2005), and *Godzilla vs. Kong*.

The repository stores generalized production grammar, not copied storyboards, frames, or film-specific copyrighted content.

## Validation Policy

Research-derived skills remain L1/L2 until they pass a real production Shot. Only validated, repeatable skills may become L3+.
